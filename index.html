About me

I'm an Automotive Engineer with interest in the areas of advanced powertrains, NVH, design of articulated multibody systems and vehicle dynamics. For three years I've pushed ideas and gained hands-on experience in designing, modeling, simulation, tuning, prototyping and testing, wringing out kinematics, dynamics, and noise until vehicles, sensors, and mechanisms behave.

I like to follow a flexible approach to problem-solving that uses limited resources in an innovative way, and I bring a passion for hands-on learning and acquiring new skills to solve complex problems. I live by the quote, "The more you know, the more you know you don't know" — Aristotle.
